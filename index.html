About Me

I'm currently a Master's student in IT Engineering at the University of Tehran, where my research focuses on the social and strategic capabilities of Large Language Models, particularly how they reason and behave strategically in complex multi-agent interactions, adapt to social contexts, and pursue goals effectively.

During my Bachelor's in Industrial Engineering, I developed a deep interest in AI, which led me to transition into IT Engineering for my Master's after achieving rank 14 in Iran's national graduate entrance exam.

As a Research Assistant at the Intelligent Information Systems Laboratory, under the supervision of Dr. Azadeh Shakery and Dr. Yadollah Yaghoobzadeh, I work on understanding and enhancing strategic reasoning and behavior in LLMs within social contexts, investigating how agents anticipate others' actions, select appropriate strategies, and adapt their behavior to achieve communicative goals in scenarios such as negotiation, collaboration, and persuasion.

My research interests broadly span LLM reasoning, strategic behavior, social intelligence, and multi-agent systems, with a focus on building AI that is not only capable, but genuinely effective in human social environments.
